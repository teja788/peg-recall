# Shorts and Reels: "Where was the peg?" clips

There are 12 vertical clips (1080×1920, H.264 with a silent AAC track, 30 fps, 17 s each), 8 in English and 4 in Hindi. The MP4s are in `~/Downloads/colorcatch-clips/` (kept out of the repo). Each clip runs like this:

1. **0–5 s, look:** a real Color Catch board during the look phase, every peg showing its color, with "Look closely… 5 4 3 2 1".
2. **5–5.6 s:** the pegs hide (the real face-down board from the same game, showing the game's own "Find <color>" banner after the die roll).
3. **5.6–9 s, question:** "Where was the RED peg?" (the color changes per clip).
4. **9–12 s, comment:** "Comment the number!" with markers 1–9 on candidate pegs and a 3-2-1 countdown. Exactly one of the nine numbered pegs is the asked color.
5. **12–14.6 s, reveal:** "It was N!" The right peg turns its color again and its number lifts and takes the peg's color.
6. **14.6–17 s, end card:** app icon, "Color Catch", "Free on the App Store", "Pass and play. No ads." No prices.

Rules for every post: never say the game improves memory, focus or brain health (no "brain training", "boost your memory" or "keeps the mind sharp"). Don't write "for kids" in a way that sounds like an App Store category claim. Only describe what's in the app.

## Pinned comment (answer)

Post the answer as a comment about **24 hours** after the clip goes live, then pin it. Commenters answer first, which helps the clip get shown more. Template:

- English: `Answer: number N. Did you get it? Color Catch is free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad (link in bio).` On YouTube, use "search Color Catch on the App Store" instead of "link in bio".
- Hindi: `जवाब: नंबर N। सही बताया? Color Catch iPhone और iPad के लिए App Store पर मुफ़्त है (लिंक बायो में)।`

Reply to a few answers, right or wrong ("So close! It was N"). Don't argue about the answer: the board in the clip is a real game.

## Link in bio

- Instagram: set the bio link to the App Store page, `https://apps.apple.com/app/id6813625311`. If you want to see which clip sent visitors, use a campaign link from App Store Connect (Analytics, Campaigns: `https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6813625311?pt=<provider id>&ct=reels&mt=8`). Use `ct=shorts` for YouTube.
- YouTube: add the same link to the channel's "Links" section (shown on the channel page), and use the Shorts "Related video" field to point to a longer gameplay video if one exists later.
- TikTok (optional): a bio link needs 1,000 followers. Until then, say "search Color Catch on the App Store".

## Making more

The clips are made from the web build (`npx expo start --web --port 8097`). A Playwright script plays a real game in headless Chromium at a 428×926 phone viewport. It saves the look-phase still, rolls until the die shows the wanted color, and saves the face-down board and the peg positions. A second script draws each frame in HTML (Nunito, with the system Devanagari font for Hindi) and pipes it to ffmpeg-static. Copies of the scripts are in `~/Downloads/colorcatch-clips/src/`. `grab-tail.mjs` is appended to the helper half of `store-assets/capture-screenshots.mjs` (everything before "the seven shots"). Ask for them to be moved into the repo if more clips are needed.
