Color Catch

Memory Chess — a wooden peg memory game for iPhone and iPad.

Colored pegs stand on a round wooden board. Look at them for a few seconds, then they all turn face-down. Roll the die, it lands on a color, and tap the peg you think is that color. Most pegs wins.

  • Play the computer at three levels: Bunny, Fox and Owl.
  • Or pass one device around for two or three players.
  • Four board sizes, from 16 pegs up to 40.
  • Color-blind-friendly palette with optional shapes on every peg.
  • Free, offline, no ads, no accounts, no data collected.
