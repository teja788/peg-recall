Color Catch · Memory Chess

Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026

Color Catch keeps no data about you. There is no account to create, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party software kits inside the app. A game on your own device is fully offline — every board, die roll, and match is computed on your device.

The one exception is Play online, which you choose to use: then the moves of that game, and the name and animal you play as, pass through our room server so that everyone in the room sees the same board. It is held only while the room is open and never stored. The details are below.

What we collect

We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information or usage information about you. We never see your email address, contacts, photos, location, device identifiers, or how you play. The only information that leaves your device is what an online game needs while it is being played, described under Play online.

Information kept on your device

The app saves a small amount of information on your device so the game remembers your preferences between sessions:

  • Your settings: sound on or off, shapes on pegs, bonus turn on a match, kid mode, board size, computer difficulty, and the animal avatars you picked.
  • Player names you type in, so they are ready for the next game. You can clear them any time with Forget player names in Settings.
  • Game statistics: wins and games played for each player name, and your record against each computer level. Reset in Settings clears them.
  • Your last chosen game mode.

This information stays on your device, inside the app's own storage. It is not readable by us, and deleting the app removes it. Only the name you choose for an online game is sent anywhere, and only to the room you play in (see below).

No accounts

Color Catch has no login, no registration, no profiles, and no cloud save. You can play the moment you open it.

Play online

Play online lets family play together on their own phones or in a browser. It is optional: nothing below happens unless you start or join a room.

  • What passes through our room server: the name you type for online play (you can leave it empty and play as your animal), the animal you picked, the room's settings, your moves, and a random seat code that lets your device take its seat back after a dropped connection. Your device also keeps that seat code and the room number until you leave or the room closes, so it can rejoin after a reload.
  • Who sees it: only the people in the same room, who joined with its 6-digit code. They see each player's name, animal and moves. There is no chat, no profiles, no friend lists, and no way to search for rooms or players.
  • How long it is kept: only in the server's working memory while the room is open. A room closes when its players leave or stop playing, and after four hours at most; everything about it is then gone. Nothing about rooms, names or games is written to a database or kept afterwards.
  • Your connection's address: like any internet service, the server sees the network (IP) address your device connects from. It is used only to run the game and to stop misuse, such as opening too many rooms, guessing room codes, or coming back to a room after the host removed you. For that, the server keeps a scrambled fingerprint of the address (never the address itself) in its working memory: for up to an hour, or for as long as that room is open in the case of a removal.
  • Hosting: the room server runs on Cloudflare, which carries the connection under its own privacy policy. We have not turned on any request logging.
  • Grown-ups in charge: the person who starts a room (the host) invites people by sharing the code, and can remove anyone from it. Report a problem, in the waiting room and in the online game's menu, opens an email to us with the room number filled in.

Online games count in the statistics on your own device, like any other game.

Color Catch on the web

The browser version works the same way: its settings and statistics stay in your browser's storage on your device, and it has no cookies, analytics or advertising. Its pages are served by Cloudflare Pages, which, like any web host, handles your IP address to deliver them. Purchases are not offered on the web.

No third-party services

The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics or crash-reporting kits, no social media kits, and no tracking of any kind. Playing on your own device makes no network requests; Play online connects only to our room server, as described above. The app asks Apple's App Store for prices and purchases (see below). On launch, the app may contact Expo's update service (u.expo.dev) to check whether a newer build of the app is available; that check contains no personal data and no gameplay data — it is standard app-update infrastructure, not tracking.

In-app purchases

From version 1.2, Color Catch offers one optional in-app purchase that unlocks the bigger boards, the Owl opponent and 3- and 4-player games, and optional tips that unlock nothing. Each one is behind a short math question meant for grown-ups. Purchases are made and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store, under Apple's own privacy policy: we never see your name, Apple Account, email or payment details. The app only asks the App Store, on your device, whether this Apple Account owns the unlock (and, for people who already had the app before 1.2, which version they first downloaded, so they keep everything free). None of this is sent to us or to anyone else, and the app still collects no data.

Children's privacy

Color Catch is a family game rated 4+ and is made for children to play with their family. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children under 13: nothing is kept, so there is no data for us to delete on request. In a game on one device there is no way for a child to contact anyone.

In Play online, a child can only play with people who have that room's code, usually family who were sent the invite. There is no chat and no messages; the only thing a player can type is their own name, which we suggest is a first name or a nickname, and which can be left empty. Names with rude words are not shown: that player appears as their animal. Rooms and names are not kept after the room closes. A grown-up can turn this off simply by not sharing room codes, and the host can remove anyone from a room.

Links out of the app: the "Rate Color Catch" button, which opens the app's own App Store page; sharing a room invite, which uses your device's own share sheet; and Report a problem, which opens your email app.

If you write to us

If you send us an email for support, we will see the email address you write from and whatever you put in your message. We use it only to answer you, and we do not add it to any list.

Future changes

Color Catch has no advertising and keeps no data about you; its only purchases are the optional unlock and tips described above, handled by Apple, and online play works as described above. If a later version ever adds advertising, or changes what online play sends or keeps, this policy will be updated and published before that version is released, and the app's App Store privacy details will be updated to match. The effective date at the top of this page always shows when the current version took effect.
