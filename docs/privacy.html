Color Catch · Memory Chess

Privacy Policy

Effective 18 September 2026

Color Catch collects no data. None at all. There is no account to create, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party software kits inside the app. The game works completely offline.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or usage information about you. We never see your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, device identifiers, or how you play.

Information kept on your device

The app saves a small amount of information on your device so the game remembers your preferences between sessions:

  • Your settings: sound on or off, shapes on pegs, bonus turn on a match, kid mode, board size, computer difficulty, and the animal avatars you picked.
  • Your last chosen game mode, and game progress or statistics stored by the app.

This information stays on your device, inside the app's own storage. It is not sent anywhere, it is not readable by us, and deleting the app removes it.

No accounts

Color Catch has no login, no registration, no profiles, and no cloud save. You can play the moment you open it.

No third-party services

The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics or crash-reporting kits, no social media kits, and no tracking of any kind. It makes no network requests to play the game.

Children's privacy

Color Catch is a family game rated 4+ and is safe for children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects no personal information from children either — including children under 13. There is nothing for a parent to opt out of, no data for us to delete on request, and no way for a child to contact anyone through the app. There is no chat, no user-generated content, no social features, and no links out of the app apart from the support page a parent may choose to open in a browser.

If you write to us

If you send us an email for support, we will see the email address you write from and whatever you put in your message. We use it only to answer you, and we do not add it to any list.

Future changes

Color Catch version 1.0 is free and has no advertising and no purchases. If a later version ever adds advertising, an in-app purchase, online play, or any other feature that touches data, this policy will be updated and published before that version is released, and the app's App Store privacy details will be updated to match. The effective date at the top of this page always shows when the current version took effect.
