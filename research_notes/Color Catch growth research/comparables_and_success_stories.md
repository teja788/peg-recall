# Comparable games and solo / tiny-team success stories (for Color Catch: Memory Match Game)

Research date: 2026-10-14. Legend: **[V]** = verified (company statement, developer's own numbers, or reputable press quoting the company); **[E]** = third-party estimate (AppGoblin, Sensor Tower, etc.); **[H]** = historical (before 2022). Estimates from AppGoblin are model-based and coarse (often only "<$10K/month").

## 1. Which comparable games (memory/matching, family board, kids', senior) are successful, and how do they monetize?

### Takeaway
The successful comparables split into two very different worlds: (a) huge free-to-play titles funded by ads + IAP (Ludo King, Solitaire Grand Harvest, Vita Solitaire for Seniors) and venture-backed kids' subscriptions (Lingokids, Pok Pok, Piknik); (b) a long tail of small, ad-free memory-match apps that earn little (AppGoblin estimates <$10K/month) but accumulate millions of installs and tens of thousands of ratings over a decade. No memory-match app found earns large revenue; the category's realistic ceiling for a solo dev looks like "steady side income with many years of compounding ratings."

### Cited Findings

**Memory / matching apps (closest comparables)**
- Memory Games with Animals (solo dev Sergio Licea, released April 24, 2013): paid/IAP unlock of image categories (Animals, Dinosaurs, Insects, Fish, Fruits/Vegetables, Dogs, Letters, Numbers), board sizes 3x4 / 4x5 / 5x6, 270+ card images, "No ads", Game Center leaderboards, universal iPhone/iPad. ~84K ratings at 4.23 stars, ~4.2M installs, ~182K MAU **[E]** — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/631738108). An earlier AppGoblin snapshot shows 82.7K ratings, 4.1M installs, ~106.5K MAU and "<$10K monthly revenue" **[E]** — [AppGoblin search snippet](https://appgoblin.info/apps/631738108). (The two snapshots differ; treat as order-of-magnitude.)
- Memory Matches 2: Brain Game: ~488.5K installs, 9.8K ratings at 4.76 stars, ~14.6K MAU, "<$10K monthly revenue" **[E]** — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/500028364)
- Memory!: ~174.8K installs, 3.5K ratings, 4.26 stars, ~4.9K MAU, <$10K/month **[E]** — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/380007120)
- Memory Game - Concentration: ~3.6K MAU, <$10K/month **[E]** — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/877559971)
- Animal Memory (preschool matching) uses a single IAP to unlock the full game, the same model as Color Catch — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/893060411)
- "Senior Games - Exercise your mind while having fun": 1.4K installs, 28 ratings at 2.82 stars, ~62 MAU **[E]** (an example of a senior-positioned app that failed) — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/885781790)

**Senior-friendly card / tabletop games**
- Vita Solitaire for Seniors (Vita Studio): 27.4M installs, ~547K MAU, ~$50K+/month revenue **[E]** — [AppGoblin](https://www.appgoblin.info/apps/com.vitastudio.solitaire). Vigor Solitaire for Seniors: ~82K MAU, <$10K/month **[E]** — [AppGoblin](https://www.appgoblin.info/apps/6478738305)
- US tabletop games (2020 data, Sensor Tower): $703.8M revenue (+40% YoY), 202.7M downloads (-12.1%). Solitaire/mahjong = $310M (44% of genre revenue) and 76.8M downloads (~37%). Top grossers: Solitaire Grand Harvest $115.3M, Solitaire TriPeaks (GSN), Yahtzee with Buddies (Scopely) **[E][H]** — [GameDev Reports summarizing Sensor Tower](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-tabletop-games-revenue)
- Solitaire Grand Harvest weekly revenue ~$2.4M–2.5M in Q4 2024; NYT Games (Wordle & Crossword) ~$1.3M/week by late Dec 2024 **[E]** — [Sensor Tower Q4 2024 solitaire](https://sensortower.com/blog/2024-q4-unified-top-5-solitaire%20games-revenue-us-600abc3f241bc16eb850508a); [Sensor Tower Q4 2024 word puzzle](https://sensortower.com/blog/2024-q4-unified-top-5-word%20puzzle%20games-revenue-us-603697f4241bc16eb8570d37)

**Family board / dice games**
- Ludo King (Gametion): crossed 1 billion downloads in 2024; modes are vs Computer, Local (pass-and-play), Online, Private rooms **[V]** — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludo_King). $20M revenue in 2020 **[V]** — [AIGF](https://aigf.in/news/ludo-kings-parent-company-clocks-20-million-in-revenue-in-2020). Details in section 3.

**Kids' apps**
- Pok Pok (Pok Pok Playroom), launched May 2021, subscription with 7-day free trial; "six figures" monthly recurring revenue, 1M+ downloads, business 5x and subscribers 9x YoY (as of June 2024); $3M seed (2022) + $6M Series A (2024) **[V]** — [TechCrunch 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/06/18/now-a-series-a-startup-kids-app-and-digital-toy-pok-pok-is-coming-to-android); [Kidscreen](https://kidscreen.com/?p=212465)
- Lingokids: ~$30M revenue around April 2021, tripled over 2020 **[V/H]**; 185M+ downloads, 30M families; raised $120M in Sept 2025 **[V]** — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lingokids)
- Toca Boca (32 apps, 140M+ downloads in 215 countries) and Sago Mini (16 apps, 13M+ downloads) were acquired by Spin Master; their bundle subscription Piknik costs $11.99/month **[V/H]** — [Mobile World Live](https://www.mobileworldlive.com/old_latest-stories/spin-master-acquires-makers-of-apps-for-kids/); [Sago Mini](https://sagomini.com/article/introducing-piknik)

### Inferences
- The closest comparable (Memory Games with Animals, ad-free, unlock categories, 3 board sizes) is nearly feature-identical to Color Catch's model and reached millions of installs mostly through 13 years of App Store search presence, not a breakout. Its signals: kid-friendly keyword in the title ("Animals"), many content packs as IAPs, universal iPad support.
- A single $2.99 unlock is in line with small memory apps but is a lower revenue ceiling than ad-funded senior solitaire (~$50K/month for Vita at 27M installs).
- "For Seniors" in the title works for Vita Solitaire (a familiar game) but not for an unknown "Senior Games" app, so the label alone doesn't bring downloads; the game itself has to be one people already search for.

### Gaps
- No public revenue for Sago Mini / Toca Boca / Khan Academy Kids in 2024–2026 was found (Khan Kids is free, nonprofit). Sensor Tower data behind paywall.
- No India-specific "memory game" chart data found; AppGoblin does not give per-country splits in what was fetched.
- IAP price lists for the top memory apps were not exposed by AppGoblin; check App Store pages directly.

## 2. Solo / tiny-team success stories with simple games: what drove growth?

### Takeaway
The repeatable drivers for tiny teams are: a built-in sharing artifact (Wordle grid), a familiar game made cleaner (Ludo King, Online Solitaire), Apple editorial/awards (Monument Valley, Pok Pok, Threes), and testing free + IAP vs premium (Threes, Card Crawl). Viral breakouts (Flappy Bird, Wordle) are not replicable on purpose; SEO/ASO for an evergreen search term plus steady polish is.

### Cited Findings
- **Wordle (2021–22, solo, Josh Wardle):** 90 players on Nov 1, 2021, about 300,000 two months later, then millions daily; growth came after adding the share-your-result emoji grid; sold to the NYT for "low seven figures" **[V]** — [Edinburgh News](https://www.edinburghnews.scotsman.com/news/world/wordle-snapped-up-by-the-new-york-times-in-low-seven-figure-deal-3550054); [Trusted Reviews](https://www.trustedreviews.com/news/wordle-sold-to-new-york-times-but-will-stay-free-to-play-4206192)
- **Online Solitaire (solo, Holger Sindbæk):** grew from $1,500/month (Jan 2020) to $10,000/month (Feb 2022), mostly ad revenue, a small part subscription **[V, self-reported]** — [Indie Hustle](https://indiehustle.beehiiv.com/p/making-10000-monthly-running-website-solitaire); later ~$25,000/month, with SEO, simplicity and design polish in a saturated market as the drivers; he notes "users not wanting to pay for apps" and the high cost of paid ads **[V, self-reported]** — [hey.gg podcast](https://www.hey.gg/podcast/holger-sindbaek-ep2)
- **Pok Pok (small team, ex-Snowman/Alto):** Apple Design Award (2021) and App Store Award for cultural impact (2023), Kidscreen Award 2024; Android expansion because "many families have Android tablets for their kids" **[V]** — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/06/18/now-a-series-a-startup-kids-app-and-digital-toy-pok-pok-is-coming-to-android)
- **Threes (2014, tiny team) [H]:** $1.99 paid, 14 months of development, Apple's iPhone game of the year 2014, cloned by 2048 within days; daily revenue doubled after a free version was released — [Daily Dot](https://dailydot.com/threes-2048-app-strategy-history); [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/-i-threes-i-devs-double-daily-income-with-free-version)
- **Arnold Rauers / Tiny Touch Tales (solo, 2021) [H-ish]:** switched premium card games to free + IAP (content unlocks, ad/timer removal, no intrusive ads) in Aug 2021: total revenue +41%, iOS downloads +280%, Card Crawl revenue +168%, Card Thief -1%; $8,600 revenue and 104K downloads Aug–Sept; an App Store feature for Gnomitaire caused a visible spike — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/marketing/item-4012). Earlier, the solitaire-style Miracle Merchant made ~$52,000 in its first month, 84% iOS **[H, 2017]** — [TouchArcade](https://toucharcade.com/2017/09/04/fantastic-solitaire-card-game-miracle-merchant-makes-52000-in-first-month/)
- **Flappy Bird (solo, 2013–14) [H]:** released May 2013, a sleeper hit in Jan 2014; developer said ~$50,000/day from ads at peak; 50M+ downloads; pulled Feb 10, 2014 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flappy_Bird); [Christian Post](https://christianpost.com/news/flappy-bird-generates-50000-per-day-in-ad-revenue.html)
- **Monument Valley (ustwo, small team) [H]:** $5.86M from 2.44M paid sales by Jan 2015; later $25M+ worldwide on a ~$1.4M budget — [MacRumors](https://macrumors.com/2015/01/15/monument-valley-ios-sales); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/monument-valley-worldwide-revenue-climbs-to-over-25-million)
- **Alto's Adventure [H]:** grossed $3.5M+; 36.5M Android downloads (Android went free with ads/IAP) — [Game Developer case study](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/alto-s-adventure-case-study); [Engadget](https://engadget.com/2018/07/17/altos-odyssey-android-release-date-free-ios-interview). **Mini Metro [H]:** 1M units by July 2017 — [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/console/-i-mini-metro-i-surpasses-1-million-sold)
- **Free-for-24h promo (solo, 2022):** Classifier made its $19.99 lifetime IAP free via Indie App Santa ($120 fee): ~10,000 installs in 24–48h, ~6,000 redemptions, covered by AppAdvice "Apps Gone Free", MacMagazine (Brazil), r/AppHookup (~200 upvotes); ratings rose from 18 to 50; DAU from ~30 to ~200; ~$600 revenue and ~$300 refunds — [Roddy Munro](https://roddymunro.substack.com/p/i-made-my-app-free-for-24-hours-was)

### Inferences
- A shareable result (Wordle's grid) is the cheapest viral loop; for a family game it could be a share card after a pass-and-play match ("Grandma beat Bunny on the 12-peg board"), without any memory or brain-health claims.
- Paid-up-front hits (Monument Valley, Alto, Mini Metro, Threes) relied on Apple featuring plus premium art and press; they're mostly pre-2018 and the market has since moved toward free + IAP.
- The Rauers and Threes data suggest free + unlock works if the free part is generous; the unlock converts better when it's content (boards, themes) than when it removes friction.
- An "apps gone free" promotion is a cheap way ($0–$120) to go from 1 rating to dozens, which matters more than revenue at Color Catch's stage.

### Gaps
- No verified 2022–2026 numbers found for Dots or 2048 (the 2048 creator didn't monetize).
- Searches turned up no specific 2023–2026 Indie Hackers / r/iOSProgramming posts with numbers for an indie kids' memory or preschool game.

## 3. Ludo King: how a dice/board family game grew in India, and lessons for an India-heavy audience

### Takeaway
Ludo King took a game "everyone has played in childhood", made the rules simpler and matches shorter, offered offline vs-computer and pass-and-play next to online private rooms whose codes were shared over WhatsApp/SMS, localized into 15 languages (which brought in players aged 45+), and grew by word of mouth with no paid marketing reported in the early phase. The COVID lockdown multiplied a base that already existed (10M DAU in 2017).

### Cited Findings
- Team of 5 at the start, 18 by Nov 2017; 55M downloads, 10M DAU, 1M new installs per day, 80% of installs from India; #1 in Google Play games from May 30, 2017; growth described as word of mouth; founder: "There were only one or two games based on [Ludo]", "Everyone has played this game in childhood", "A good game can be made with a smaller team, less resources" **[V/H]** — [PocketGamer.biz 2017](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/interview/67033/how-a-small-indian-studio-got-10-million-daus/)
- Launch date conflict: Wikipedia gives Feb 20, 2016, PocketGamer.biz says Dec 2016. Gametion's earlier Snakes and Ladders game underperformed before Ludo King **[V]** — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludo_King); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/interview/67033/how-a-small-indian-studio-got-10-million-daus/)
- Download milestones: 50M (May 2017), 120M (Sep 2017), 300M (Jan 2020), 400M (Jun 2020), 500M (Dec 2020), 800M (Jun 2022), 1B (2024) **[V]** — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludo_King)
- Monetization: about 80% ads / 20% IAP at first, shifting to 60/40 by June 2020; ~50% of revenue from India; $20M revenue in 2020 (4x growth); May 2020 revenue ~$922K **[V/E]** — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludo_King); [AIGF](https://aigf.in/news/ludo-kings-parent-company-clocks-20-million-in-revenue-in-2020)
- Lockdown 2020: DAU from 13–15M pre-COVID to a 51M peak; servers scaled from 8 to 200+; team of 20; peak hours 2–4 pm and 8–11 pm; core audience 18–34 — [afaqs](https://www.afaqs.com/news/mktg/ludo-king-vikash-jaiswal-on-how-ludo-became-indias-no-1-lockdown-game). Wikipedia lists a "peak DAU 251 million", which conflicts with 51M from the founder and looks like an error. Daily downloads rose from 150K (Feb) to 450K+ (Apr 2020), with 26M downloads in April 2020 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludo_King)
- 400% growth in 2020; DAU 15M→32M and MAU 110M→142M over nine months — [afaqs](https://www.afaqs.com/news/mktg/ludo-king-grew-400-in-2020-vikash-jaiswal-gametion-technologies)
- 15 languages (English, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Marathi, Spanish, German, French, Arabic, Indonesian, Italian); attracted a new 45+ demographic, partly thanks to Hindi/Gujarati/Marathi support; Quick mode matches take ~5 min vs 30–90 min for traditional Ludo **[V]** — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludo_King); [afaqs](https://www.afaqs.com/news/mktg/ludo-king-grew-400-in-2020-vikash-jaiswal-gametion-technologies)
- Private room codes are shared via WhatsApp, SMS or email; offline modes are vs Computer and Local pass-and-play; up to 6 local players; voice chat added Aug 2020 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludo_King)
- Features that came from users: "Play online" was added after requests; simplified rules vs competing Ludo games; "dice pad" interface and soundtrack; themes unlocked via missions; one 2018 marketing campaign with singer Mika Singh — [afaqs](https://www.afaqs.com/news/mktg/ludo-king-vikash-jaiswal-on-how-ludo-became-indias-no-1-lockdown-game)
- Awards: Google Play Best Casual Game 2018; Apple's "21 apps for 2021" — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludo_King)

### Inferences
- Color Catch already has three Ludo King traits (dice roll, vs computer, 2–4-player pass-and-play). What it lacks: an invite or share mechanism (WhatsApp share of a result or a "play this with me" link), Indian regional languages beyond Hindi (Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam), and a hook based on a familiar game name. Ludo King's 45+ growth came through local-language support.
- Ludo King's revenue is ad-led (60–80%), and India's revenue per user is low; a $2.99 unlock is a big ask in India. Regional IAP pricing (e.g. ~INR 99) is more realistic. Monetization in India is likely to stay small; India is better treated as a ratings/download base.
- Most of Ludo King's scale came from Android (Google Play charts); an iOS-only app reaches a smaller, wealthier slice of Indian users.

### Gaps
- No primary source quantifies how much of Ludo King's growth came from WhatsApp sharing specifically; that link is inferred from the room-code feature and the word-of-mouth description.
- Ludo King's iOS-only numbers for India were not found.

## 4. What top "memory game" apps do in listing, monetization and features

### Takeaway
Long-lived top memory apps share a pattern: a plain, searchable title with a subject keyword ("Memory Games with Animals", "Memory Matches"), multiple board sizes, many themed card sets sold as IAP or unlocks, no ads (a selling point for parents), leaderboards, and universal iPad support. Their rating counts (3.5K–84K) built up over 10+ years.

### Cited Findings
- Memory Games with Animals: description leads with adjustable difficulty (3x4, 4x5, 5x6), "over 270 different card images", 8 themed categories, "No ads", Game Center leaderboards, iPhone + iPad native, sound toggle; ~84K ratings — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/631738108)
- Memory Matches 2: Brain Game (4.76 stars, 9.8K ratings) uses "Brain Game" in the title — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/500028364). (Color Catch must not use brain/memory-improvement claims; a descriptive subtitle such as "color dice & peg memory game for families" is the safe alternative.)
- Most memory-match apps use IAP and/or ads, and most AppGoblin revenue estimates are under $10K/month — [AppGoblin search results](https://appgoblin.info/apps/492916965)
- Animal Memory: one IAP unlocks the whole game — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/893060411)

### Inferences
- Content packs (more peg themes, e.g. animals, fruits, vehicles shown as shapes on pegs) are the most common unlock in this category and could be added to the $2.99 unlock or sold as small IAPs.
- "No ads" is worth stating in the first lines of the listing and in screenshots, since parents search for it.

### Gaps
- Live App Store rankings for "memory game" in the US and India (positions, IAP price lists) were not fetched; that needs App Store pages or the keyword-research skill / ASC data.

## 5. Patterns a solo dev can realistically copy on a small budget

### Takeaway
What can be replicated: a familiar game concept in a searchable title, a generous free core plus a content unlock, a built-in share or invite loop, localization into the audience's own languages (regional Indian languages for India), ratings prompts at happy moments, cheap "free for a day" promotions, pitching Apple editorial, and many years of steady updates. Not replicable: lockdown-style surges, viral randomness (Flappy Bird), venture-funded kids' subscriptions.

### Cited Findings
- Word-of-mouth growth with no paid marketing and a 5-person team (Ludo King 2017) — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/interview/67033/how-a-small-indian-studio-got-10-million-daus/)
- Localization into regional languages pulled in 45+ players (Ludo King) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludo_King)
- Share artifact took Wordle from 90 to 300K players in two months — [Edinburgh News](https://www.edinburghnews.scotsman.com/news/world/wordle-snapped-up-by-the-new-york-times-in-low-seven-figure-deal-3550054)
- SEO + polish of an existing classic took a solo dev from $1.5K to $10K/month in two years, then ~$25K/month — [Indie Hustle](https://indiehustle.beehiiv.com/p/making-10000-monthly-running-website-solitaire); [hey.gg](https://www.hey.gg/podcast/holger-sindbaek-ep2)
- Free-for-a-day promo: ~10K installs and ratings 18→50 for $120 — [Roddy Munro](https://roddymunro.substack.com/p/i-made-my-app-free-for-24-hours-was)
- Switching to free + content IAP raised iOS downloads 280% and revenue 41%; one App Store feature produced a visible spike — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/marketing/item-4012)
- Apple awards/featuring were central for Pok Pok, Threes, Monument Valley — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/06/18/now-a-series-a-startup-kids-app-and-digital-toy-pok-pok-is-coming-to-android); [Daily Dot](https://dailydot.com/threes-2048-app-strategy-history)
- Paid ads are expensive for solo devs (Sindbæk) — [hey.gg](https://www.hey.gg/podcast/holger-sindbaek-ep2)

### Inferences (candidate tasks)
1. Add a share/invite loop: an end-of-match result card or "play with me" message designed for WhatsApp (India) and iMessage.
2. Localize into more Indian regional languages (Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam) along with a regional listing; check INR regional pricing for the unlock.
3. Add themed peg/content sets (animals, fruits) as part of the unlock and say so in the listing; mention "No ads" in the first line and screenshots.
4. Run a free-unlock promotion (e.g. via AppAdvice / r/AppHookup / an indie promo event) mainly to collect ratings; add an App Store review prompt after a won match.
5. Submit to Apple's "Get featured" form with an accessibility angle (shapes for color-blind players, no ads, no data collected, 18 languages).
6. Expect slow compounding: comparables like Memory Games with Animals took about 13 years to reach about 84K ratings.

### Gaps
- Conversion rates for one-time unlocks in kids' or senior memory apps: no public data found.
- Whether an Android version would matter (Ludo King's and Pok Pok's data suggest families in India often use Android) can't be quantified for this app from public sources.
